What each layer means
PM₂.₅: five-minute AirGradient readings. Latest AQ uses corrected values where available; Historic AQ uses the original raw co-location record. Unit 5 is indoors and must be read on its own scale.
Wind: exact-time roof-sensor data for Latest AQ; a regional 10 m estimate for Historic AQ. The rose points downwind, so its tail marks where the wind comes from. The shaded sector could carry emissions from 173 towards the terrace.
Plume: illustrative, and only drawn during the assumed 16:00–00:00 opening (peak 18:00–22:00). It is not a measurement, CFD result or proof of operation. A visible plume is documented only for 173; the other outlets stay off unless selected.
Geometry: EA 1 m LiDAR context combined with OSM and supplied photographs. Installed host homes and mounts are photographically confirmed; outlet locations, instrument heights and plan positions remain approximate rather than surveyed.
Context only: the A201 animation uses a 2022 traffic count and the monitoring analysis uses SWK06 as an urban-background reference. Neither is a modelled concentration surface.
